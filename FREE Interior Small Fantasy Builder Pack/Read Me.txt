FREE INTERIOR SMALL FANTASY BUILDER PACK
Nimbekit | Version 1.0 | 1,000 catalogued elements | 16px construction grid

Build welcoming houses, busy taverns, royal chambers, libraries, workshops,
prisons, underground vaults, and magical rooms from one coordinated interior kit.

CONTENTS
200 walls: 10 materials x 20 pieces, including all 16 cardinal-neighbour patterns
and four solid face details per material.
200 floors: 10 materials x 20 pieces, including all 16 border-neighbour patterns
and four interior alternatives per material.
200 furniture designs for homes, inns, shops, workshops, chapels, and palaces.
150 interior props including food, crockery, books, plants, rugs, windows and doors.
150 dungeon elements including stairs, columns, traps, cages, remains and treasure.
100 arcane objects including crystals, potions, runes, portals and alchemy equipment.

The count includes modular connections, material families, colour variants,
and open/closed states. Repeated exports in atlases or maps are not added to the
1,000 count. All 1,000 individual PNGs have distinct rendered pixel data.

START HERE
1. Open Asset Browser.html in a browser. It works offline with no installation.
   Search by name or four-digit ID, filter categories, inspect sizes, or download PNGs.
2. Import Sprites into your project, or use the Atlases folder with its JSON frames.
3. Set nearest-neighbour filtering, turn off smoothing, and build on a 16px grid.
4. Open any Examples/*.tmx map in Tiled to inspect an editable sample interior.
5. Read the licence before redistributing a finished project.

FILE GUIDE
Sprites: 1,000 individual PNG files in six category folders, numbered 0001–1000.
Atlases: six category sheets, a combined 48px-slot sheet, and JSON frame metadata.
Tiled: a mixed-size image collection and separate 16px wall and floor tilesets.
Examples: three editable TMX rooms plus their native 512x352 PNG renders.
Previews: five listing screenshots and six labelled category contact sheets.
Documentation: JSON/CSV catalogue, palette, technical guide, and licence.

SIZES AND PIXELS
709 assets are 16x16. Larger assets use 32x16 (66), 16x32 (130), 32x32 (84),
16x48 (3), or 32x48 (8) canvases. Sizes include transparent padding.
PNG sprites have binary alpha: every pixel is fully visible or fully transparent.
No text labels, grids, or preview backgrounds are baked into the game sprites.
The 48px atlas slots are a packing format, not a different tile scale.
All artwork is static. There are no animation frames, sounds, characters, or game logic.

EXAMPLE ROOMS
Hearthwood Tavern: main room, kitchen and guest room.
Moonvale Library and Chambers: library, royal bedroom and craft room.
Underkeep Arcane Dungeon: vault, portal chamber, prison and alchemy room.
These are example layouts, not playable games. Collision and game interactions
