# Building recipes

These recipes use asset IDs from `Data/Asset-Index.csv`. All placements are on a 16×16 grid. Put surfaces and building walls on the Architecture layer and doors, windows and trim on Details.

## Timber cottage

1. Make a five-tile-wide, two-tile-high facade using plaster panel 001, with 005 for a few crossed beams.
2. Build a roof area above it using terracotta tile 101. Tile 105 supplies a lower eave; 110 is a dormer accent.
3. Put oak door 141 on the lower middle wall cell. Add windows 151 and 158 at opposite ends.
4. Place chimney 161 near the roof edge and step 191 in front of the entrance.
5. Add barrel 201, flower basket 213, apple sapling 411 and flower cluster 451 around the house.

## Woodland workshop

Use oak wall panels 011–020, wooden roof pieces 131–140, door 143 and window 154. Place workbench 263, anvil 261, stacked planks 266, logs 267, chopping block 268 and tool chest 270 nearby. Use signs 281/282 for an entrance and a small lantern 285 beside the path.

## Market square

Lay pale cobbles 521, then add awnings 197–199 above stalls. Add bread 241, cheese 242, apples 243, fish 244, vegetables 245 and cloth 248. Use well 291 or basin 288 as a focal point, with lanterns 285 at the corners. All stalls and goods remain individual tiles you can rearrange.

## Kitchen garden and orchard

Use soil 515 or furrows 516/517 under crops 461–480. Enclose with fence 171/172 and gates 179/180. Alternate fruit trees 411–418, then add watering can 256, seed bag 257 and wheelbarrow 258. Flowers 441–460 make small borders; hedge 435 and corner hedge 436 add structure.

## Enchanted summer grove

Start with darker grass 507 and moss 508. Place compact trees 401–430, shrubs 431–440 and ferns 486/487. Set a forest altar 333 on stone paving 521 or mosaic 594, then add crystals 321–324, rune stone 325, fairy lantern 381 and mushrooms 481–485. Use ruined columns 361–363 to frame the clearing.

## Terrain placement

IDs 531–540 provide selected dirt path segments; 551–558 provide selected shore edges/corners. Inspect their catalog before placement. The set is intended for manual construction, with optional rotations or flips in your own editor when useful. Variants differ in shape and detail; they are not a complete automatic transition system.
